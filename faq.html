Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my team ranked here?
The ranking reflects the output of the TOP 50 Engine. Specific placement depends on a large number of factors.

Can I appeal my team's ranking?
All rankings are final at the time of publication. Rankings are reviewed before each update.

How often is the ranking updated?
Periodically.

What does the Rating mean?
Higher is better. See the Methodology.

Why is my team not on the list?
The list contains 50 teams.

What is a Class?
A grouping of teams by rank. See the Methodology.
